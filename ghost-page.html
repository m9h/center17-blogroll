Annual anchors

  • Computational Psychiatry Course, Zurich (TNU, ETH/UZH): annually in early September; 2026: 7–12 Sept, hybrid the starting block
  • SOBP Annual Meeting and Brainhack: annually late April / early May; 2026: 30 Apr–2 May, New York Society of Biological Psychiatry
  • Aaron Swartz Day: annually around 8 November, Internet Archive, San Francisco open access, open data; see the suicide-prevention resources below
  • OHBM Annual Meeting: annually in June
  • Brainhack Global: annually in December

Suicide prevention

Kept alongside Aaron Swartz Day. If you are in crisis, use the first line that applies to where you are.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741 (US)
  • International Association for Suicide Prevention: crisis centres by country
  • Samaritans (UK and Ireland): 116 123
  • American Foundation for Suicide Prevention: research funding and resources
  • Zero Suicide (Education Development Center): health-system framework
